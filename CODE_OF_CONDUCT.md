# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Cooee to be safe, respectful, and productive.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experiences
and viewpoints, accept useful feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behaviour is inappropriate, unsafe, or
harmful to the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
private where practical, and mindful of the safety of people reporting an
incident.

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@cooee.sh`. Do not include
sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to investigate. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This code applies in all project spaces and to public conduct undertaken on
behalf of the project.
