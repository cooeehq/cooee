## Design Context

### Users

Cooee is used by product and engineering teams to turn shipped work into customer-facing changelog updates. Operators need to publish quickly without designing each post manually; readers need updates that are clear, credible, and easy to scan on desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality

Subtle, polished, and technical. Cooee should feel quietly capable and opinionated, with warmth coming from each changelog's brand accent rather than loud decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined developer-tool restraint: calm editorial layouts, greyscale imagery, controlled depth, and selective brand colour. Raycast is a reference for finish and visual confidence, not for copying its identifiable artwork. Avoid generic AI graphics, prominent accent borders, busy patterns, and decorative effects that compete with changelog content.

### Design Principles

- Content is read before decoration is noticed.
- Brand colour belongs inside artwork as a tint, overlay, or highlight—not as a loud perimeter.
- Typography must remain legible across photographic, abstract, light, and dark imagery.
- Prefer a small number of deliberate treatments over many weak variants.
- Mobile reading space and predictable interaction states are first-class constraints.
- Nested card surfaces use concentric corners: an inset surface’s radius equals its parent radius minus the parent inset, so media and nested elements sit cleanly within their card.
